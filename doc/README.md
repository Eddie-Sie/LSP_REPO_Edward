# CSCI 363 – Large Scale Programming

## Assignment #1 – Course Orientation

### 1. Are late programming assignments accepted?

No. Late assignments are not accepted. All required assignments must be submitted by the specified due date and time.

### 2. Where are official course announcements posted?

Official course announcements, resources, and assignment updates are posted on **Piazza**. Students are responsible for monitoring Piazza regularly.

### 3. How are exam dates announced?

Exam dates are announced **in class and posted on Piazza at least two weeks in advance**. Students are responsible for monitoring these announcements and attending the scheduled exams.

### 4. When is a makeup exam considered?

A makeup exam may be considered only for an **extraordinary circumstance beyond the student's control**. The student must notify the instructor as soon as possible and provide appropriate supporting documentation.

### 5. Is AI permitted?

AI tools such as ChatGPT, GitHub Copilot, Claude, and Gemini may be used **only when permitted by the instructor or for a specific assignment**. When AI is permitted, students are still responsible for the accuracy, originality, testing, documentation, understanding, and defense of their submitted work.

### 6. Which course policy will most impact your success, and why?

The policy requiring students to submit assignments on time will have the greatest impact on my success. Since late assignments are not accepted, I need to manage my time carefully and make sure I complete and submit my work before each deadline.

### 7. What are the office hours for LSP Fall 2026?

Office hours are available **as needed by Zoom appointment**. Students should contact the instructor to arrange an appointment.

### 8. Who is responsible for monitoring Piazza, and what types of updates may be posted?

Students are responsible for monitoring Piazza regularly. Piazza may contain course announcements, resources, assignment updates, clarifications, and other important course information.

### 9. What documentation is needed if you are unable to take an exam because of a medical or approved circumstance?

Students must notify the instructor as soon as possible and provide supporting documentation that verifies their inability to participate. The documentation does not need to include a diagnosis or private medical information. A doctor's note or urgent-care receipt does not automatically guarantee approval; the instructor evaluates the circumstances.

### 10. Does scholarship status entitle a student to different grading, deadline, or attendance requirements?

No. The syllabus does not state that scholarship status provides different grading, deadline, or attendance requirements. The course requirements and policies apply to students in the course. Students are expected to participate, complete required work by the deadline, and meet the course requirements.
